## Entry 21 — 2026-09-11

can you help me achieve this task : The hard part: how long does a customer stay?
`customer_lifetime_months()` is not in any file in `data/`. It is not in the brief. Nobody gives it to us, and without it there is no lifetime value and no LTV:CAC ratio.
It has to be derived backwards. `marketing_funnel_monthly.csv` contains estimated LTV figures for the existing markets, around €137–139, alongside the acquisition cost of about €44. Those LTV figures were produced at Dutch, Danish and Swedish prices — which we know, because `historical_sales_weekly.csv` gives units and revenue per week per country, and revenue divided by units is the average price actually achieved there.
From a known lifetime value and a known price, you can solve for how many months of purchasing that value implies. Once you have that number, hold it fixed across the German price scenarios: we are testing what happens when the price changes, not assuming customers also become more or less loyal at the same time.
Write the derivation out in the docstring — the inputs you used, the arithmetic, the result. A grader should be able to follow it without reading the code. And do not round it to something convenient: a suspiciously tidy "12 months" with no working shown is exactly the kind of invented number the brief warns against.
